FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Is ClimaSearch free to use? +
Yes, ClimaSearch is completely free. You can use it as a guest without creating an account. Creating a free account unlocks streak tracking, XP progression, outfit saving, and achievements.
How accurate is the weather data? +
ClimaSearch uses WeatherAPI.com, a professional weather data provider that updates conditions every hour using satellite data, weather stations, and meteorological models. Coverage includes over 3 million locations worldwide.
Can I use ClimaSearch without creating an account? +
Absolutely. Guest mode lets you search any location and see outfit recommendations instantly, without signing up. Creating an account is optional — it just lets you save progress, outfits, and streaks between sessions.
How do I save outfit items? +
Once you've searched a location and your outfit cards appear, simply tap or click on any clothing card to save it to your Outfit Journal. Saved items are tagged with the city and time, and appear in the journal below the search results.
What cities and countries are supported? +
ClimaSearch supports thousands of cities, towns, and postcodes across every country. Try major cities like London, New York, Tokyo, Dubai, Sydney, Paris, Berlin, and many more. You can also enter postcodes or smaller towns.
Is my personal data safe? +
Yes. Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 before storage and are never readable by anyone, including us. Your data is stored locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage. We don't sell data to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

About ClimaSearch

The story behind your smart weather & outfit companion.

ClimaSearch was built with a simple idea: nobody should ever leave the house underdressed or overdressed. We combine real-time weather data with practical clothing advice to help you dress right for every day.

What is ClimaSearch?

ClimaSearch is a free, web-based weather and outfit recommendation tool. You enter any city, town, or postcode, and we instantly show you the live temperature, humidity, wind speed, and "feels like" conditions — then suggest exactly what to wear based on that data.

Unlike a basic weather app, ClimaSearch translates weather into actionable outfit advice, covering everything from extreme cold gear (balaclava, snow boots, down parka) to hot weather essentials (tank tops, flip-flops, sun hats).

Features

  • Real-time weather — Live data for thousands of cities worldwide via WeatherAPI.com
  • Outfit recommendations — Clothing suggestions across 7 temperature bands
  • Outfit Journal — Save favourite picks, tagged by city and time
  • Streak system — Build daily search streaks to stay motivated
  • XP & Levels — Earn experience points and level up as you explore
  • Achievements — Unlock badges for milestones like your first search, 3-day streaks, and more
  • Guest mode — Use the app without creating an account
  • Secure accounts — SHA-256 password hashing, local data storage

Our Mission

We believe useful tools should be free, fast, and respectful of users' time and data. ClimaSearch is ad-supported to keep it free for everyone. We aim to stay independent, lightweight, and focused on doing one thing exceptionally well.

Privacy Policy

How ClimaSearch collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: March 2025 · Applies to climasearch.co.uk

1. Who We Are

ClimaSearch ("we", "us", "our") is a free weather and outfit recommendation web application operated by John Adetunji, based in the United Kingdom. Our website is accessible at climasearch.co.uk.

2. What Data We Collect

ClimaSearch collects minimal data. Here is exactly what is stored:

  • Account data (optional): If you create an account, we store your name, email address, and a SHA-256 hash of your password. Your actual password is never stored or readable by anyone.
  • App progress data: Your XP level, streak count, saved outfits, and achievement status are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Location searches: The city or postcode you type is sent to WeatherAPI.com to retrieve weather data. We do not log or store your search queries.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

ClimaSearch does not use tracking cookies. We use your browser's localStorage to save your session and app progress on your own device. This data does not leave your device and is not accessible to us.

Google AdSense, which powers advertisements on this site, may set cookies to serve personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ad Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

  • WeatherAPI.com — Provides real-time weather data. When you search a location, the location string is sent to WeatherAPI.com. See their privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense — Displays advertisements to support the free service. Google may collect data per their privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — Loads fonts (Space Mono, DM Sans). Google may log the request. See Google's privacy policy.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above solely to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • Retrieve weather data for the location you search
  • Display personalised outfit recommendations
  • Track your streak, XP, and achievements locally in your browser

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All account and progress data is stored in your browser's localStorage. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser storage for climasearch.co.uk. Signing out removes the session from localStorage. There is no server-side database storing your personal information.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

As a UK user you have the right to access, correct, and delete any personal data we hold. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. If you have questions about your data, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed using SHA-256 via the browser's Web Crypto API before storage. Plain-text passwords are never stored anywhere. We recommend using a unique password for your ClimaSearch account.

9. Children's Privacy

ClimaSearch is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
